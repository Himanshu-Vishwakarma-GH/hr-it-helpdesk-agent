# Account Lockout

Category: IT — Accounts & Access

Your account locks automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts. This is a
security rule and IT cannot disable it.

What to do:
1. Wait 15 minutes — most lockouts clear on their own after 15 minutes.
2. After the wait, sign in with the correct password. If you are not sure of
   it, reset it first at the self-service portal instead of guessing again —
   every wrong guess restarts the 15-minute lock.
3. If the account is still locked after 30 minutes, raise a ticket with
   category "Account Lockout" and mention the time of your last attempt.

Lockouts are often caused by an old password saved on a phone or a second
laptop. After you change your password, update it on every device. If you
keep getting locked out even with the right password, tell IT — it usually
means a saved old password somewhere is retrying in the background.
