# Leave Policy

Category: HR — Leave

Full-time employees get the following paid leave every calendar year:

- Earned leave: 18 days per year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Unused earned
  leave up to 10 days carries over to the next year; anything above 10 days lapses.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year. No approval needed for a single day, but a medical
  certificate is required if sick leave runs for more than 2 consecutive days.
- Casual leave: 6 days per year for short personal work.
- Public holidays: 10 days per year as per the company holiday calendar.

How to apply: submit the request in the HR portal at least 3 working days before
the leave starts for earned and casual leave. Your manager approves it in the
portal. For leave longer than 5 working days, add a handover note naming who
covers your work. Leave cannot be taken during your notice period except for
sick leave. Unused casual leave does not carry over.
