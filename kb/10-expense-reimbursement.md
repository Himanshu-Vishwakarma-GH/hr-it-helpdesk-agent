# Expense Reimbursement

Category: HR — Finance

Work expenses are reimbursed once a month. The process:

1. Submit the claim in the HR portal within 30 days of the expense, with the
   original bill or invoice attached. Claims without a bill are rejected.
2. Travel by cab for client visits is reimbursed at actuals. Food during
   client visits is reimbursed up to Rs. 500 per day.
3. Internet reimbursement for work-from-home days is a flat Rs. 500 per month
   — no bill needed for this one.
4. Claims are approved by your manager first, then by Finance. Payment lands
   in your salary account by the 10th of the next month.

Expenses that are never reimbursed: personal subscriptions, traffic fines,
alcohol, and any expense above Rs. 5,000 without prior manager approval.
If a claim is rejected, Finance writes the reason on the claim itself — fix
that point and resubmit instead of raising a new claim.
