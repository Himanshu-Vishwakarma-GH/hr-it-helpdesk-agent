# Printer Issues

Category: IT — Hardware

If the office printer is not working:

1. Check the printer display for an error. The most common issues are paper
   jams, low toner, and the paper tray being empty — all three are fixed at
   the printer itself, no ticket needed.
2. If your laptop cannot find the printer, remove it from your printer list
   and add it again from the Company Printer Portal.
3. If a print job is stuck, cancel it from your print queue and try again.
   Do not keep re-sending the same job — it creates a queue jam.
4. For confidential documents, use secure print: send the job, then release
   it at the printer with your employee badge.

If the printer shows a hardware error code or makes a grinding noise, stop
using it and raise a ticket with the printer name (on the sticker on its
side) and the error code. Mention the floor — there are two printers per floor.
