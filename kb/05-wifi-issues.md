# Office Wi-Fi Issues

Category: IT — Network

If the office Wi-Fi is slow or keeps disconnecting:

1. Forget the network "Company-WiFi" on your device and reconnect with your
   work credentials. The network name is case-sensitive.
2. Check whether colleagues nearby have the same problem. If everyone is
   affected, it is a network outage — check the IT status page before raising
   a ticket.
3. Move closer to an access point. Meeting rooms on the far side of the floor
   have weaker signal; the rooms near the pantry have the strongest.
4. If only your laptop is affected, restart the Wi-Fi adapter: turn Wi-Fi off,
   wait 10 seconds, turn it back on.

Guest Wi-Fi ("Company-Guest") is for visitors only and is slower by design.
Never share your own Wi-Fi credentials with visitors — ask reception for a
guest pass instead. If the problem continues after these steps, raise a ticket
with your floor and desk number.
