# Password Reset

Category: IT — Accounts & Access

If you forgot your work password or it has expired, reset it yourself from the
self-service portal at https://password.company.internal — no need to raise a ticket.

Steps:
1. Open the portal and enter your work email address.
2. Choose "Send reset link" and check your inbox (also check spam).
3. The link is valid for 30 minutes. Pick a new password of at least 12 characters,
   with one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
4. Sign in again on all devices. If you use VPN, reconnect with the new password.

If the reset link never arrives, or your account is locked after too many failed
attempts, contact the IT helpdesk and mention "password reset — no email received".
Password resets do not need manager approval.
