# Laptop and Hardware Requests

Category: IT — Hardware

To request a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, or headset:

1. Raise a ticket with category "Hardware Request" and state the item, the reason
   (new joiner, replacement, or upgrade), and your delivery address.
2. Standard issue is one laptop and one monitor per employee. A second monitor
   needs your manager's approval — add your manager in CC on the ticket.
3. Approval usually takes 1–2 working days. After approval, IT ships within
   3–5 working days to office addresses, or 5–7 working days to home addresses.
4. For a broken device (cracked screen, battery swelling, keys not working),
   choose "Hardware Repair" instead and describe the fault. Do not try to open
   the laptop yourself — that voids the warranty.

Accessories under Rs. 2,000 (mouse, cables, laptop stand) can be ordered directly
from the office supplies portal without a ticket. Anything above that needs a
ticket and approval.
