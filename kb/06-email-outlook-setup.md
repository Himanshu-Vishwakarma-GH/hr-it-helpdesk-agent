# Email and Outlook Setup

Category: IT — Accounts & Access

New laptops come with Outlook pre-installed. To set up your work email:

1. Open Outlook and sign in with your work email address and password.
2. When prompted for MFA, approve the sign-in on your authenticator app.
3. Mail syncs automatically. Your mailbox size limit is 50 GB — if you hit it,
   archive old mail to a local folder or delete large attachments.
4. Shared mailboxes (like support@ or careers@) appear automatically within
   2 hours of access being granted. If a shared mailbox is missing, raise a
   ticket with the mailbox name and your manager's approval.

Email rules: never forward work email to a personal address, and never share
your password with anyone, including IT. If you get a suspicious email asking
for credentials, do not click any link — forward it to phishing@company.internal
and delete it.
