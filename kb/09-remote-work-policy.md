# Remote Work and Work From Home Policy

Category: HR — Work Policy

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Rules:

- Mark your WFH days in the HR portal calendar by Monday 10 AM for that week.
- You must be reachable on chat and email during working hours (10 AM – 7 PM).
- Video-on for team standups is expected unless your manager says otherwise.
- If your home internet goes down for more than an hour, inform your manager
  and either come to the office or take the rest of the day as leave.
- Working from a different city for more than 5 working days needs HR approval
  in advance. Working from outside the country always needs HR approval.
- WFH is not available during the first 30 days of joining, or during the
  notice period.

Laptops taken home are covered by the same IT policy as in the office — keep
the device locked when you step away, and do not let family members use it
for personal work.
